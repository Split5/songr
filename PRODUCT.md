# Söngr

## Product truth

Söngr, known internationally as Soengr, is a private Navidrome and Subsonic
client. The listener owns the
library and the server; there is no third-party recommendation account,
advertising profile or remote catalogue.

## People and setting

The primary listener uses Söngr alone on an iPhone, often with one hand and
often while doing something else. The app is opened to continue a record,
find something specific, or keep a favourite available offline. It is not a
social product and it has no growth, engagement or administration dashboard.

## Jobs

1. Continue the current listening session without losing the queue.
2. Pick a record from recent, new, familiar or similar music.
3. Search the complete local library quickly.
4. Curate favourites and playlists.
5. Download music and understand exactly what remains available offline.
6. Change server, playback and recommendation settings without exposing
   technical implementation as the main experience.

## Product rules

- The album artwork is the strongest visual material.
- Navidrome is the only recommendation and library source.
- Playback state must remain visible across every top-level surface.
- Swedish and English use the same layout and never expose untranslated
  implementation terms.
- Offline, empty, loading and error states are part of the interface.
- Physical iPhone audio behaviour remains the final proof for playback.
