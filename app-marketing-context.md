## Value proposition

- **Problem:** People who keep music on a private Navidrome or Subsonic server
  often need several tools for playback, offline listening, filtering,
  playlists, lyrics and queue control.
- **Target audience:** Self-hosting music listeners, privacy-conscious iPhone
  users, Navidrome and Subsonic users, and people who own a large local music
  library.
- **Unique differentiator:** A calm, native-feeling client built around a local
  catalogue with offline playback, detailed filtering, smart server-side
  mixes, lyrics and an optional setup checklist.
- **Elevator pitch:** Söngr brings your own music server to iPhone without ads,
  tracking, a developer account or a subscription.

## Product principles

- The user connects to a server they own or can access.
- Optional Navidrome plugins improve some features, but the main player,
  library, playlists, downloads and search keep working without them.
- The app sends no listening data to the developer.
- The password stays in Apple Keychain. Server URL and username stay on the
  device.
- No demo credentials, server addresses or personal library data belong in a
  published build.

## Goals

1. Publish a trustworthy first version that passes App Review.
2. Reach Navidrome and Subsonic users searching for an iPhone client.
3. Convert privacy-focused self-hosters with clear product screenshots and
   honest optional-plugin messaging.
4. Reach a stable 4.5+ rating after launch by prompting after successful use,
   not during first-run setup.
